# Dataset: Analysis of Estonian buildings EPCs

## Description

This dataset contains the data regarding the residential buildings in Estonia, coupled with their respective Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) ratings. This dataset is the resulting output of a comprehensive processing pipeline combining building geometries and Land Surface Temperature (LST) derived from Landsat 8/9 and SDGSAT-1 images to support predictive modeling and clustering of energy efficiency classes.

## Variables Description

The tabular dataset `epc-estonia.gpkg` contains the following key columns:

### Identifiers & Target
*   `id_building` (Integer): Unique identifier for the building, derived from the Estonian Building Registry (`ehit_id`).
*   `geometry` (Polygon): Geometry of the building (`EPSG:3301`)
*   `energy_class` (String): String variable representing the energy efficiency performance (Classes A-H).

### Geometric & Spatial Features
*   `area` (Float): Area of the building footprint in square meters (m²).
*   `perimeter` (Float): Total perimeter length of the building footprint in meters (m).
*   `ap_ratio` (Float): Area-to-perimeter ratio.
*   `boundary` (Float): Total length of the shared boundary with adjacent/touching buildings (m).
*   `boundary_ratio` (Float): Ratio of the shared boundary length to the total perimeter.
*   `ax_max` (Float): Length of the major axis of the building's minimum rotated bounding rectangle (m).
*   `ax_min` (Float): Length of the minor axis of the building's minimum rotated bounding rectangle (m).
*   `ax_ratio` (Float): Ratio of the minor axis to the major axis.

### Land Surface Temperature (LST) Features
Values represent temperature statistics in Celsius (°C). Metrics are available for winter (`_winter` suffix) and summer (`_summer` suffix) from the Landsat (`_landsat` suffix) and SDGSAT-1 (`_sdgsat` suffix) images:
*   `lst_mean_building_<season>_<mission>` (Float): Mean LST calculated directly over the building footprint.
*   `lst_std_building_<season>_<mission>` (Float): Standard deviation of LST over the building footprint.
*   `lst_mean_sourround_<season>_<mission>` (Float): Mean LST calculated in a 150-meter buffer surrounding the building, explicitly masking out other building footprints.
