# T2.3.1 - Remote Sensing Analysis

## Description

In This folder we collect the results of the remote sensing analysis

- `epc-estonia`. Analysis of Estonian residential buildings EPCs
- `pv-estonia`. Analysis of Estonian residential buildings Installed PV
- `lst-ferrara`. Analysis of Building LST in Ferrara


## Acknowledgements of Original Sources

This datasets were generated by harmonizing and processing several raw data sources. We gratefully acknowledge the providers of the original data:

*   **Building Registry Data & EPCs.** Extracted from the Estonian national building registry e-ehitus platform 

    > Maa- ja Ruumiamet [Estonian Land and Spatial Development Board]. Ehitisregister (EHR) [Estonian Building Registry]. e-ehitus platform. https://livekluster.ehr.ee/ui/ehr/v1/

* **Aerial Images.** Extracted from the Estonian Geoportal

    > Maa- ja Ruumiamet [Estonian Land and Spatial Development Board]. Geoportal. Orthophotos. https://geoportaal.maaamet.ee/est/ruumiandmed/ortofotod-p99.html 

* **Landsat 8-9 thermal imagess.**

    > Earth Resources Observation and Science (EROS) Center. (2020). Landsat 8-9 Operational Land Imager / Thermal Infrared Sensor Level-2, Collection 2 [dataset]. U.S. Geological Survey. https://doi.org/10.5066/P9OGBGM6

* **SDGSAT-1 thermal images.**

    > International Research Center of Big Data for Sustainable Development Goals (CBAS). (2026). SDGSAT-1 Thermal Infrared Spectrometer (TIS) data [dataset]. SDGSAT-1 Open Science Program. http://www.sdgsat.ac.cn/ 

* **Municipality of Ferrara.**
    * Winter Night Image https://dati.comune.fe.it/dataset/thermal-images-20233001 
    * Summer Day Image https://dati.comune.fe.it/dataset/thermal-images-20230716-temperatura-delle-superfici 
    * Summer Night Image https://dati.comune.fe.it/dataset/thermal-images-20230717-temperatura-delle-superfici
